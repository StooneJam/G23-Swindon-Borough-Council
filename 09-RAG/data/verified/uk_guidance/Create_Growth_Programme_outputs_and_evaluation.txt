Key outputs and stories from the Create Growth Programme which has provided over £43 million in funding to help businesses build their investment readiness and connect with investors.
This page contains resources and case studies for creative entrepreneurs, businesses or regional leaders interested in finding out more about government support for creative businesses growth journeys. A map of finance is included that provides signposting of funding options for creative start ups and scale ups.
Create Growth Programme
The Create Growth Programme (CGP) has helped thousands of businesses across the country to build their investment readiness and connect with investors. CGP has developed a model for support, including access to mentors, expert guidance on presenting business plans to financiers, and recognising and registering IP and copyright, alongside connections to suitable investors.
The Create Growth Programme has provided over £43 million in DCMS funding since it began:
- Almost £16 million has been provided to the 12 regions involved to deliver regional investment readiness support, with over 900 businesses completing cohorts to date
- Innovate UK has awarded almost £21 million in grant funding to over 430 creative businesses, with more than £5 million in private finance leveraged as match against this
- The programme has also facilitated creative businesses to engage in international showcase events such as SXSW (Austin, Texas), and Slush (Helsinki, Finland), and those much closer to home (eg Climb)
- And worked to increase investor capacity for the creative industries by developing relationships with both regional and national investor networks, with examples of success stories of CGP supported businesses that have gone on to achieve investment
More details about CGP can be found via the Innovate UK Business Connect website. See also the list of Innovate UK CGP competition winners.
This experience has laid the groundwork for future investment beyond the programme, and is informing the next phase of investment readiness support such as improved signposting, industry’s work to create a single front door, and the Creative Places Growth Fund.

Creative Industries finance map
This map provides an overview of the current financial sources available to creative businesses at different stages of growth.
It reflects a snapshot in time and will be further developed through industry-led work to develop a ‘single front door’ for creative firms to access information on how to unlock private investment as outlined in the Creative Industries Sector Plan.
Please note this is a new version of the map (last updated 31 March 2026).
We are keen to hear your feedback on the map. Send us an email at creategrowth@dcms.gov.uk with your suggestions for how it could be improved.
Use your browser’s zoom setting to view the map or download the PDF.
